# Changelog

From 1.0.0 on, every release is listed on this repository's Releases page, with notes taken from
its pull request. The `CHANGELOG.md` inside each release zip has the full history.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-17

First release.

- The minimap rim takes the season's colour, blending into the next season over two weeks, with
  painted seasonal accents and slowly drifting snowflakes, leaves and petals. Snowy lands have
  their own wintry set; hot lands get a dry season instead of winter.
- Frost on cold mornings, using an exact copy of ImmerseMe: Weather's temperature model.
- A season arc under the minimap with the four seasons, today's place in the year and a notch for
  every festival; festivals that are on now glow.
- A short transition when you travel to another land.
- A tooltip with the date (Year 25 after the Dark Portal), sunrise and sunset, the frost, the
  festivals ahead and the solstices.
- Commands: `/ims` (or `/seasons`); `/ims help` lists them all.
